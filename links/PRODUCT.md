# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a small JS file, generated by `build.mjs` from `links.json` (no framework). Deploy target: Vercel or Netlify with a custom domain (e.g. links.marcotulio.com.br). Confirmed in the original brief.

## Users

Primary: business owners and salespeople who reach the page from the Instagram bio of @mtuliobarbosa, almost always on a phone, within seconds of seeing a post. They are deciding whether Marco Túlio is someone worth talking to about their sales problem.

Secondary: followers who want more of his content (podcast, social profiles) and event organizers looking to invite him to speak.

## Product Purpose

Marco Túlio's personal link-in-bio page, replacing the BuddX Linktree. It must lead with him as a person, then his companies. Success = a business owner opens a WhatsApp conversation with him (the single commercial path). Getting to know him (podcast, socials) is the supporting path.

## Positioning

A person, not a catalog: an entrepreneur who shares what he learns building 4 companies (AI/CRM, marketing, consortium, aesthetics). It must not read as an agency page or a list of companies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Main page: max 4 cards, exactly one highlighted (the WhatsApp CTA), no card without a title, no embedded video in the list.
- Cards, in order: podcast, WhatsApp "Quero conversar sobre meu negócio" (highlight), Minhas empresas (subpage /empresas), Palestras e convites.
- Subpage /empresas: compact header + 4 companies (The Seller, BuddX, Alvo3, Veelure) with UTM `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio`.
- All copy and links live in `links.json`; missing data shows a visible [PREENCHER] marker and is never invented.
- Analytics hook (Plausible or GA4), off by default. Open Graph + favicon.
- Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 in Performance and Accessibility; must load fast on 4G.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Marco Túlio, with verified badge. Subtitle "Empreendedor · IA e Vendas". Bio: "Empreendedor. Compartilho o que aprendo construindo 4 empresas."
- Binding visual constraint from the user: The Seller palette (dark navy background, electric blue accent, white text), always dark; a large cut-out photo of him over a brand-colored background at the top, round avatar with accent ring.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese, informal and direct ("Me conta seu desafio de vendas").

## Evidence on Hand

- Only the brief's content: name, bio, subtitle, the 4 companies and their one-line descriptions.
- Media/talks: the user will list real events, interviews or outlets. Until then, show none.
- Photo: the user is sending one in this conversation. Company logos: not provided.
- Absent and never to be fabricated: numbers (followers, clients, episodes), testimonials, client logos, press.

## Product Principles

1. Person first, companies second.
2. One commercial path, unmistakable; everything else supports it.
3. Understood in a few seconds on a phone.
4. Real or nothing: missing data stays visibly missing instead of being invented.
